# What Spanish and Filipino segments already pay for — and whether a "Gran's budget" opens the door (13 Sep 2026)

## Spanish (Spain / Mexico / US Hispanic)
- Senium (ES, web): PRO €9.90/mo, FAMILY €19.90/mo — carer coordination, med reminders + dose history, family chat,
  reports. Praised: "menos discusión familiar, más claridad y evidencia real"; "antes WhatsApp y calendarios… ahora hay
  trazabilidad". NO expense feature. senium.es
- Tend – Cuidados Coordinados (es-MX): $4.99/mo — shift notes, meds, team roles. No expenses, no ratings yet.
- CircleCare (US, Spanish-localised): $6.99/mo — shared calendar, med adherence, feed. No expenses; tiny traction.
- Aleda (Basque, free beta): the ONLY Spanish-language app with "Gastos: quién ha pagado qué… se acaban las tensiones
  por el dinero entre hermanos". Pre-revenue.
- Kinnect.club ($79/yr) tells US–MX siblings to use shared spreadsheets + a joint Mexican bank account.
- Demand evidence: AARP Español + Family Caregiver Alliance both prescribe shared sibling expense records; Latino
  caregivers spend $7,167/yr = 47% of income (Latinas 56%) vs 26% overall.

## Filipino / OFW
- No Filipino elder-care or family-coordination app exists.
- GCash/Maya: bills-pay-for-family, send-money, kid allowances — nothing for elders; GCash pitches bills-pay as
  "eliminate the worry of whether money sent home is being used for the right purposes".
- BeamAndGo: in-kind vouchers built explicitly as "solution to misuse of pera-padala" ("I sent money for medicine,
  but one of my brothers used some of it for gambling").
- Field experiments (NBER 20839; De Arcangelis & Yang 2022 UAE): merely LABELLING remittances for a purpose raised
  amounts sent by >15%; hard enforcement added ~2%. Visibility, not control, moves senders.
- 51% of OFW households spend remittances on medical expenses (BSP CES Q1 2025).
- Current workaround: notebooks, shared Google Sheets, weekly "money sync", "<₱500 needs no approval" rules.

## Cross-cutting
- Nobody mainstream has expense tracking (Caring Village, Neela, Medisafe, CircleOf…); the ones that do are tiny:
  CareSplit (iOS launch Apr 2026, 314-family waitlist), Givers (caregiver debit card, 10 ratings), eLivelihood (50 installs).
- "People pay for this" is unproven anywhere.

## Verdict
A shared "Gran's care ledger" is a DIFFERENTIATOR for the OFW segment and a wedge for the Spanish segment — IF built as a
LABELLED LEDGER (sender logs "sent ₱4,000 – meds"; sibling logs "bought ₱3,850 – Losartan, receipt photo"; monthly total;
optional simple settle-up; NO money movement), not a Splitwise clone. Prior "split-the-bill" was removed for good
reason; this is different — it is about visibility of what was sent for Gran, which the research says is the thing that
lifts remittances.
The most visibly requested feature GranWatch lacks in both segments: a hired-carer role (yaya / cuidadora) with shift
notes and "who was there" — it is Tend's whole pitch and Senium's PRO tier.
