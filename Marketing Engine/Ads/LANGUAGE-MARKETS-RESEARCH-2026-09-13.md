# Language-market research for the first paid Meta ads (13 Sep 2026)

Purpose: choose the first 3 language+region segments for paid Meta ads, testing the
hypothesis that the best buyer is the "distant child" (moved away, feels guilty, often
sends money home, has relatives near the parent who could visit).

## Cross-cutting
- Meta targeting still available (per 2026 third-party guides, not verified in Ads Manager):
  Behaviours > Expats (Philippines / Mexico / South Africa / ...); life events "Recently moved",
  "Long-distance relationship", "Away from hometown" ("Away from family" not confirmed — treat as gone);
  language targeting by Facebook UI language. Detailed-targeting EXCLUSIONS were removed in 2025 —
  age caps still work (how to stop the algorithm buying 65+ women again).
- CPM consensus (vendor estimates, ±2x): PH $1.5–3.5 · BR $2.6–4.2 · MX $2–4.5 · ZA $2.5–4.2 ·
  ES $5.5–8 · PT $4.5–6.5 · UAE $6.5–10 · FR $7–10 · NL $7–9.5 · DE $8–11 · UK $10–13 ·
  CA $11–14.5 · AU $11–18.5 · US $16–23. CPC: PH $0.28 · BR $0.35 · MX $0.45 · ZA $0.65 ·
  ES $0.85 · FR $1.15 · UAE $1.40 · DE $1.45 · UK $1.95 · US $2.69.
- 65+ share of population 2025: PH 5.7% · ZA 6.9% · MX 8.5% · BR 11.5% · US 18.4% · UK 19.7% ·
  NL 20.9% · ES 21.6% · FR 22.5% · DE 23.7% · PT 24.9% · UAE 1.8%.
- Gulf caveat: 2026 Iran conflict has disrupted Dubai (flights, some expats left) — audiences and
  prices volatile; "I can't fly home right now" is itself a trigger.

## Key facts per segment (sources in the chat research of 13 Sep)
- Filipino: 2.19M OFWs (57% women; KSA 22%, UAE 12%; 88% remit), stock incl. permanent ≈ UAE 0.8M,
  US 4.6M, KSA 865k, CA 957k, AU 409k, HK 187k, SG ~200k; remittances $35.6B (2025); FB 90.8M (78%);
  Play PH accepts GCash/Maya/ShopeePay; strongest filial norm (utang na loob). No competitor found.
  Trigger: Language=Filipino OR Expats (Philippines) + location UAE/KSA/HK/SG; age 25–50.
- Spanish: 10.9M Mexican-born in US, $66B remittances, median age 47 (parents now 70+); 2.6M LatAm-born
  in Spain; MX FB 93M; Play MX takes cards/carrier billing/Mercado Pago (OXXO cash not for subs);
  familismo + legal alimentos duty. Competitors: Senium (ES), Tend/CircleCare (US). Trigger:
  Language=Spanish + Expats (Mexico) + location US; age 30–55.
- Brazilian PT: 5.18M abroad (US 2.09M, PT 513k, JP 210k); cheapest big market; IG 147M > FB 109M;
  Play BR accepts Pix/PicPay/Mercado Pago; filial care is a LEGAL duty (Estatuto da Pessoa Idosa).
  pt-BR is WRONG for native Portuguese.
- Afrikaans: 6.37M L1; ~1.0M SA abroad (UK 245k, AU 213k, US 162k, NZ 102k); Afrikaans as FB
  language ≈ perfect SA-origin proxy; "semigration guilt". Trigger: Language=Afrikaans + UK/AU/NZ/UAE;
  age 30–55.
- French: 2.8–3.2M abroad; Famileo 260k paying families (~€14M rev) = proof of willingness to pay for
  a grandparent link; Quebec wants its own copy.
- German: FB weekly use 31% and falling; 32% trust US providers; Elternunterhalt only >€100k income;
  Nui Care exists. Weak.
- Dutch: 1.6M with a parent 80+, 605k giving care, 5.5M mantelzorgers; FB flat (9.9M); Caren is a
  provider portal not a family app. Angle = overloaded carer, not distance.
- English Gulf: UAE 88% expat (IN 4.4M, PK 1.9M, PH 0.8M, UK ~240k); 65+ only 1.8%; no Hindi/Malayalam
  in the app, so Indian resonance weaker than the numbers suggest.

## Recommendation
Test first: (1) Filipino OFWs in UAE/KSA/HK/SG, (2) Spanish — Mexico-born in the US,
(3) Afrikaans diaspora in UK/AU/NZ/UAE (sharp, affluent contrast to two remittance segments).
Brazilian PT is the close fourth (swap for Afrikaans if scale > contrast).
Copy hypotheses: PH "You send the money. Who does the visiting?" / "Nasa abroad ka, pero hindi
mag-isa si Nanay."; MX "Mandas dinero cada mes. ¿Pero sabes quién fue a ver a mamá esta semana?";
AF "Jy's in Perth. Ma is in Bloem. Wie was laas by haar?"
